Try out Engelbart

We’re both former students involved in the EA and AI safety communities. We created Engelbart to help students like you build their first side projects. Working on our own side projects while in school helped to spark our own interests in HCI and AI safety and think it is super beneficial to work on a project of your own when trying to make an impact or learn about a new field. It used to be quite difficult to code projects independently since you would have to know many programming languages, but it's now far less esoteric with AI; you can communicate, plan, and design a project using just plain language.

Engelbart is an open-source research workbench and free to EA and AI safety students at Berkeley, so if you'd like to get started on a project to build up your resumé and begin making an impact within EA and AI safety, we'd love for you to check out Engelbart! If you'd like additional help, feel free to schedule a meeting below! We can also get you set up with $25 of Claude Code credits to help you build your first project!
